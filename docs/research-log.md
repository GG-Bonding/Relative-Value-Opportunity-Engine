# Research log

## 2026-10-01 — Laboratory, not a market history

Hypothesis: a book that fades a residual can harvest a lag that is actually in the price, and the same book will stop when that lag collapses to one session.

Dataset: seeded laboratory, business days from 2010 through 2022, macro history from 2008 so surprises have a past. Price equals a structural log fair value lagged five sessions until 2021-01-01 and the contemporaneous fair value after that, plus small noise. Three permanent price steps sit inside the no-lag window. No CFTC or option rows are written.

Model: rolling ridge, 504-session window, 252 minimum, ridge penalty 1, embargo of one session. Features are the registered relative factors and expected relative fundamentals. Own-price momentum is excluded.

Result on the laboratory tape: the book trades while the five-day lag is the price process and records no new entries once the reaction-lag gate marks alpha `DECAYING`. Fills pay the quoted bid or ask. The after-cost pnl is positive inside the lag window. The exact counts and the run hash are in `artifacts/v1-research-report.md`, which is regenerated by `rv research-report`.

Conclusion for this hypothesis, on this tape: the machinery behaves as specified. Conclusion for live EURGBP: **INCONCLUSIVE**. The lag was written into the generator. Vendor vintages are not in the repository.

## Experiments that are kept

`always-long-laboratory` and `fundamental-sign-is-the-trade` are appended to `artifacts/experiments.jsonl` by the report command. A constant long, and a book that trades the sign of the rate level directly, are not the residual fade. Their close-to-close pnl is stored whether or not it is worse. A worse result is `REJECTED`. A result that is not worse is `INCONCLUSIVE`. Neither is deleted.

## Not tried

Kalman or any dynamic beta. A second pair. A hidden Markov regime. A model that scans features for Sharpe and then names a mechanism. Each fails the complexity gate until EURGBP has a stable out-of-sample result on revision-safe vendor data.
